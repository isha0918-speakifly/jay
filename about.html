J N Shah & Associates, Chartered Accountants

Overview

J N Shah & Associates is a Chartered Accountants firm based in Gujarat, India. We provide accounting, audit, taxation, compliance and advisory services to businesses across various industries.

Supported by a team of accounting and compliance professionals, we serve businesses across multiple sectors and are continuously expanding our capabilities in cloud accounting and technology-enabled accounting services.

Our focus is on building long-term professional relationships through quality-driven accounting support and efficient processes.

  • Established: 2023
  • Team size: 11–50 members
  • Industry: Business consulting and services
  • Platforms: Xero, QuickBooks Online

Offshore accounting support

Alongside our current professional practice, we are developing a dedicated offshore accounting support vertical assisting CPA firms and accounting firms with bookkeeping, reconciliations, month-end close support, financial reporting and audit support.
